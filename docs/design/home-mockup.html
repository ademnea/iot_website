IoT-ra (Internet of Things - Research & Applications) Lab

Connecting the world, one device at a time.

We design low-cost connected sensors for Uganda's apiaries, farms and power networks, and train the engineers who build and maintain them.*

What we're building

Every project starts in the field with the people who will use it: beekeepers, farmers and utility engineers.*

OngoingApiaries

Smart hive monitor

Sensors inside the hive report weight, temperature and sound so beekeepers know when to inspect.*

OngoingComputer vision

Bee detection

A camera model that counts bees at the hive entrance and flags unusual activity.*

OngoingMobile

Beekeeper's app

Puts hive readings and alerts on the beekeeper's phone, built with farmers in the field.*

OngoingData

Apiary analytics platform

One place to compare colonies, seasons and sites across the lab's deployments.*

PastClimate

IoT weather station

A low-cost station on a custom circuit board, logging local weather for nearby farms.*

PastEnergy

Power monitoring

Measures supply quality and energy use, with a matching power-supply module for field kits.*
