# Fix 013: Public site redesign in the IoT-ra brand

- **Report IDs:** W11, W12, W17 (public pages), W10, W14 (public header); D12 (old CDN libraries)
- **Date:** 2026-10-21
- **Approved design:** [docs/design/home-mockup.html](../design/home-mockup.html), published for review

## What changed for visitors
- **Brand:** colours come from the lab's logo (`public/img/logo.jpeg`, "IoT-ra Smart
  Connections"): mesh navy `#034486`, signal blue `#0C9CF2`, and honey orange `#FE8800`
  for status labels only.
- **Layout like the former site:** a white page and header between navy bands (the contact
  strip, the page banner and the footer).
- **The home banner** shows the logo's Makerere tower drawn as a network. Data packets climb
  it and the signal mark pulses; it stays still for visitors who reduce motion.
- **Type:** Sora for headings, Heebo for body text, IBM Plex Mono for small data labels
  (dates, venues, status).
- **Menu:** Home · Projects · **Research** (was "Products") · News · **Team & contact** (was
  "About Us") · Admin login. The anchors the old menu linked to still work: `/#vision`,
  `/#mission`, `/#objectives`, `/#word`, `/projects#ongoing`, `/projects#past`,
  `/publications#reports`, `/publications#datasets`, `/publications#products`,
  `/about#researchers`, `/about#partners`, `/about#contactus`.
- **Footer:** only real links (Projects, Research, News, Partners, Gallery, Contact us),
  the lab's contacts, and whichever social links the admin has entered. The dead "Privacy
  Policy / Terms / FAQs / Cookies" items and the newsletter box that went nowhere are gone
  (W11).
- **Empty sections are hidden** from visitors, for example a role group with no members
  (W12).
- **Contact form:** proper labels, and the visitor's text is kept after an error. The page
  returns to the form to show the error or the thank-you message (W10).
- **Speed:** public pages load one 13 KB stylesheet (3.6 KB compressed) built by Vite.
  Before, they loaded about 27 files from CDNs: Bootstrap 5.0 and 5.3, Tailwind 2, jQuery,
  Owl Carousel, WOW and a loading spinner (W17).

## What did not change (business processes)
- **Code:** no routes, controllers, models or database changes. Every page renders exactly
  the data its `show_data` method already passed.
- **Admin workflows:** unchanged. See [docs/workflows.md](../workflows.md).
- **Grouping still follows the stored status.** Projects and events are grouped by their
  saved status (Ongoing/Past); nothing is inferred from dates.

## Placeholders, not sample content
Nothing lab-specific is hard-coded. Text, photos, people, partners, contacts and links all
come from what admins enter.
- **Visitors:** when something hasn't been added, the section is hidden, or a short neutral
  note appears ("No projects have been published yet.").
- **Signed-in admins:** they also see a dashed hint, "*Vision* hasn't been added yet. Add it
  in the admin area", linking to the page where it's added
  (`resources/views/components/admin-hint.blade.php`).
- **Logo:** an uploaded logo is always used. Until one is uploaded, the header shows the
  lab's own IoT-ra logo from the repository (`public/img/logo-iot-ra.png`, a transparent copy
  of `public/img/logo.jpeg`).
- **Banner:** the banner photo admins upload sits under the navy overlay of the home and page
  banners.
